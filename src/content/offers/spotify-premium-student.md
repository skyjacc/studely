Spotify Premium Student is **half price** for verified higher-ed students. You get the full Premium experience for the cost of a coffee.

## What you get
- Ad-free, on-demand music and podcasts
- Offline downloads and higher audio quality
- In some regions, bundled extras (e.g. an ad-supported streaming add-on)

## How to claim
1. Go to [spotify.com/student](https://www.spotify.com/student/).
2. Verify your enrolment through SheerID.
3. The discount applies to your existing Spotify account.

## Good to know
You can renew the student rate up to a set number of years (commonly 4) while you stay verified. Bundled extras vary by country.
