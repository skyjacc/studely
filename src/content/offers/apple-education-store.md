The Apple Education Store offers **lower prices on Mac and iPad** for students. During the back-to-school season, you can often get a **free gift card or accessory** on qualifying purchases.

## What you get
- Education pricing on most Mac and iPad models
- Discounted AppleCare+
- Seasonal promos (gift cards / free accessories) at certain times of year

## How to claim
1. Visit the [Apple Education Store](https://www.apple.com/us-edu/store) for your country.
2. Confirm eligibility (often via UNiDAYS student verification).
3. The education price shows automatically for eligible products.

## Good to know
Discount amounts and the back-to-school promo vary by region and season. Verification may be requested after purchase.
