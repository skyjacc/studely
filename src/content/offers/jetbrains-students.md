A free JetBrains student licence gives you the **full professional versions** of every JetBrains IDE. These are the same paid tools used in industry, at no cost while you study.

## What you get
- IntelliJ IDEA **Ultimate**, PyCharm **Professional**, WebStorm, CLion, Rider, DataGrip and the rest
- The AI Assistant and all Pro features
- Works on Windows, macOS and Linux

## How to claim
1. Apply at [jetbrains.com/community/education](https://www.jetbrains.com/community/education/#students).
2. Verify with a university email, an ISIC card, or official enrolment documents.
3. Activate through your JetBrains Account or the Toolbox App.

## Good to know
The licence renews yearly while you remain eligible. If you graduate, you keep a perpetual fallback licence for the version you had.
