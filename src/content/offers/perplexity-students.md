Perplexity regularly runs **student campaigns that make Pro free** (or a full year free) for verified `.edu` students and participating campuses. It helps with research, citations and coding.

## What you get
- Perplexity Pro: more powerful models, unlimited Pro searches, file uploads
- Better sourcing and follow-up reasoning for coursework and research

## How to claim
1. Check the current student offer on [perplexity.ai](https://www.perplexity.ai/) (look for the students / campus page).
2. Verify with your school email.
3. Some campaigns require signing up before a deadline or inviting classmates.

## Good to know
Student AI offers change often and vary by region. Confirm the exact current terms on Perplexity's site before relying on it.
