Autodesk gives students **free education licences** to its professional design and engineering software: the same tools used across architecture, product design and film.

## What you get
- Fusion 360, AutoCAD, Maya, 3ds Max, Revit, Inventor and more
- Full professional features under an education licence
- Access for the duration of your eligibility

## How to claim
1. Go to [autodesk.com/education](https://www.autodesk.com/education/edu-software/overview).
2. Create an Autodesk account and verify academic status via SheerID.
3. Download and activate the products you need.

## Good to know
Education licences are for **learning, not commercial work**. They renew while you stay verified. Re-confirm your status yearly.
