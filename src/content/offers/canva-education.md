Canva for Education gives eligible schools **Canva Pro-level tools for free**: premium templates, the Background Remover, Magic tools, and a brand kit.

## What you get
- Thousands of premium templates and design elements
- Background Remover and Magic design tools
- Class and group collaboration features

## How to claim
1. Visit [canva.com/education](https://www.canva.com/education/).
2. Verify as a teacher, or join as a student via a teacher invite or eligible institution.
3. Access applies once your school is verified.

## Good to know
Eligibility for higher-ed students varies by country and institution. If Canva for Education isn't available to you, students can still get a free Pro trial.
