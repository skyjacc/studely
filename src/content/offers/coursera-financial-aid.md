Coursera lets you **apply for financial aid on most paid courses**. If approved, you take the full course and earn the certificate **for free**.

## What you get
- Full access to graded assignments and the shareable certificate
- Works on individual courses (and many Specializations, course by course)

## How to claim
1. Open a paid course and choose **Financial aid available** below the Enroll button.
2. Fill in the short application (why you need aid, how the course helps).
3. Approval typically takes about 15 days; then enroll for free.

## Good to know
You apply per course, and there's a short waiting period. Auditing many courses is also free if you only need the material, not the certificate.
