Azure for Students gives you **$100 of cloud credit with no credit card**. It's one of the easiest ways to host projects, run VMs and try managed databases for free.

## What you get
- $100 Azure credit, valid for 12 months
- Free tiers of popular services (App Service, Functions, databases)
- Free access to select developer tools and learning paths

## How to claim
1. Go to [azure.microsoft.com/free/students](https://azure.microsoft.com/free/students/).
2. Sign in with your school account and verify academic status.
3. No payment method is required to start.

## Good to know
Renewable yearly while you stay a verified student. If you have the GitHub Student Pack, additional Azure benefits may stack.
