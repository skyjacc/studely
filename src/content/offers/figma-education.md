The Figma Education plan gives students the **Professional tier for free**: unlimited files, version history, team libraries and dev-friendly handoff.

## What you get
- Unlimited Figma design files and projects
- Shared team libraries and advanced prototyping
- FigJam for whiteboarding and planning

## How to claim
1. Go to [figma.com/education](https://www.figma.com/education/) and apply.
2. Verify your student/educator status via SheerID.
3. The plan applies to your account and any education team you create.

## Good to know
Verification lasts about a year; you'll be asked to re-verify to keep the plan. Great paired with the design skills in the GitHub Student Pack.
