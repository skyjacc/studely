Google Cloud's free offering combines a **$300 trial credit** (90 days) with an **always-free tier**. That's enough to host side projects, run a small VM, or learn managed services.

## What you get
- $300 in credit for the first 90 days
- Always-free tier: a small e2-micro VM, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions quota and more
- Occasional extra credits through Google's student and cloud-skills programs

## How to claim
1. Start at [cloud.google.com/free](https://cloud.google.com/free).
2. Add a card to start the $300 trial (you won't be auto-charged when it ends).
3. Check your institution for Google Cloud student / skills-boost credits.

## Good to know
The trial needs a card for identity, but Google does not auto-bill when the trial ends. You choose whether to upgrade.
