The GitHub Student Developer Pack is the best first stop for any student who codes. One verification gets you **100+ partner offers**: cloud credits, free Pro tiers, domains, learning platforms and more.

## What you get
- **GitHub Copilot Pro** free while you're a verified student
- Free credits on DigitalOcean, Microsoft Azure and Heroku alternatives
- A **free `.me` domain + SSL** via Namecheap
- Free tiers of JetBrains, Sentry, Datadog, and dozens more

## How to claim
1. Sign in to GitHub with the email your school issued.
2. Go to [education.github.com/pack](https://education.github.com/pack) and click **Get student benefits**.
3. Verify with SheerID (school email or proof of enrolment).

## Good to know
Approval is usually instant with a `.edu`-style email; otherwise upload proof of enrolment. Benefits refresh while you stay verified, so re-verify each year.
