Good for a portfolio or first project: a **free `.me` domain for a year** plus a **free SSL certificate**, delivered through the GitHub Student Developer Pack and Namecheap's student program.

## What you get
- One free `.me` domain registration for 12 months
- A free SSL certificate to secure your site
- Standard Namecheap DNS management

## How to claim
1. First get the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack).
2. From the pack, open the Namecheap offer (or go to [nc.me](https://nc.me/)).
3. Register your `.me` domain and claim the SSL.

## Good to know
The first year is free; renewals are paid at normal rates, so set a reminder if you don't want to keep it. One domain per verified student.
